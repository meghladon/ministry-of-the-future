↳ Why a share-house round, and not the tax one

  • The demo is about the method, not the topic. A technical question about super tax buries what we're trying to show: that an algorithm can find agreement underneath disagreement.
  • Everyone has an instant opinion about housemates. No expertise required. No "let me think about that" — agreement and disagreement come fast, which is what a small-room cluster map needs.
  • vTaiwan started this way too. Their first big rounds were Uber and online alcohol sales — concrete, lived experiences with clear stakeholders. Tax came later, once the method had proven itself.
  • The clusters here are real. Cleanliness vs vibes. Communal vs autonomous. Rules-people vs trust-people. Your cluster map will likely show two or three groups — and the bridges between them are what the room secretly agrees on.
